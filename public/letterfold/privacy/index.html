Privacy Policy

Letterfold does not collect any data.

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your progress and settings stay on your device and are erased when you delete the app.

On first launch iOS may download Apple's built-in Songti font from Apple; this is done by the system and the app sends no information about you. The falling petals follow the tilt of your device; that motion is read in the moment, never stored, and never leaves the device.

Questions: haoqianglyu@gmail.com. Any change to this policy will be posted on this page.
